# Phonk beat – prompty pro Gemini (hudba)

Hotové soubory ulož do `assets/hudba/` jako `phonk_<nazev>.mp3` nebo `.wav`. Beat jde potichu pod řeč streamera a zesílí na pointě a pod kartou se skóre.

Verze 2 (15. 11.): první verze byla „moc elektronická“. Edity na TikToku znějí špinavě a „sampleovaně“: páskové zkreslení, lo-fi bicí, přeladěné vokální výkřiky bez slov, Memphis atmosféra. Proto prompty níž chtějí špínu, vokální chopy bez textu a zpomalený, temný zvuk místo čistých syntezátorů.

## 1) Phonk edit (hlavní)

Gritty phonk for a viral TikTok edit, 120 BPM, minor key. It should sound sampled and dirty, not clean or synthetic: tape-saturated, slightly lo-fi, like an old Memphis rap record chopped up. A dark, catchy cowbell melody that repeats and gets stuck in your head, a huge distorted 808 that slides and rumbles, hard-hitting dusty drums, rattling hi-hats. Chopped and pitched-down vocal shouts and grunts as rhythm elements, with no real words or lyrics. Aggressive, confident, "aura" energy that makes a clip feel cooler. Keep the middle of the mix a little open so a person talking over it is still understandable. No intro, starts at full energy, loops cleanly, no fade-out.

## 2) Slowed cinematic edit (temné, „villain“)

Slowed and reverbed dark phonk for a cinematic movie-style edit, about 80 BPM, minor key. Heavy, haunting and emotional: a slow eerie bell or music-box melody, deep distorted 808 slides, booming slowed drums with a big snare, washed in reverb, warm tape hiss and vinyl crackle. Pitched-down, wordless vocal textures in the background. It should feel like a dramatic slow-motion moment. No lyrics. Leave space in the middle for speech. Starts immediately, loops cleanly, no fade-out.

## 3) Brazilian funk edit (chaos, faily)

Brazilian funk phonk for a hype TikTok edit, 130 BPM. Bouncy, raw and distorted: punchy funk kick pattern, overdriven 808, a short repetitive cowbell or whistle hook, crowd-like wordless chants and vocal chops (no lyrics), claps and shakers. Party energy, a little crazy and funny. Gritty and saturated, not clean electronic. Leave space in the middle for speech. Starts at full energy, loops cleanly, no fade-out.
